# Tells - web (AVP copy review)

Concrete markers for the `tells` skill. Every entry has three fields only: Marker, Why it is a
tell, The question. A pattern named by the allow-list in `SKILL.md`, `docs/design-system.md` or
`docs/design-direction.md` is not a tell. A mood word names nothing. Facts-only applies to every
number, name and logo (`CLAUDE.md`).

Sources: observations of landing pages generated by current models with no design guidance, and
the tells catalogue of taste-skill (Leonxlnx, MIT), as rewritten in the upstream genjutsu
module.

## Invented information

### Weather or locale strip
- **Marker:** a city code, a clock and a temperature together in a header or footer (`LIS 14:23 · 18°C`), or a timezone badge like `GMT+1`.
- **Why it is a tell:** models add it to make a company feel inhabited; the values are chosen for atmosphere, not read from anything.
- **The question:** does AVP need to show a place or time to this visitor, and which system provides it?

### Fake build status
- **Marker:** release strings and sync times on a page that is not a changelog: `v0.6.2-rc.1`, `last sync 4s ago`, `Build 0048`, `BETA`.
- **Why it is a tell:** it borrows the texture of developer tools; the numbers are made up and stay frozen.
- **The question:** which real release or `FORMULA_VERSION` does this track, and where does the page read it?

### Fake heritage
- **Marker:** a founding year worn as a badge: `ESTD. 2018`, `Since 2016`.
- **Why it is a tell:** an established date is the quickest borrowed trust, so models invent one.
- **The question:** is this AVP's actual founding year, and does the visitor need it?

### Fake part numbers and section codes
- **Marker:** codes stamped on sections or panels that point to no catalogue: `SEC-01`, `MK.I`, `REF-204`, `UNIT 07`.
- **Why it is a tell:** a code makes a page look engineered by borrowing a spec sheet's authority.
- **The question:** which real document, scan id or catalogue does this code point to?

### Fake stock counter
- **Marker:** scarcity or live counts: `Only 3 spots left`, `27 agencies viewing`, `412 of 800 claimed`.
- **Why it is a tell:** urgency is a conversion reflex; with no system behind it the page makes a claim it cannot keep.
- **The question:** which table or service provides this number, and does it change when the underlying data does?

### Fake photo credit
- **Marker:** captions that credit nobody real: `Plate 03 · House archive`, `Field study no. 12` under stock or placeholder images.
- **Why it is a tell:** captions make a placeholder look curated; the photographer and archive do not exist.
- **The question:** who made this image, and does AVP have the right to credit them?

### Placeholder names and brands
- **Marker:** `John Doe`, `Jane Smith`, `Acme`, `Nexus`, `lorem ipsum`, testimonials signed by people never asked, sample brand names in the report or demo that read as real customers.
- **Why it is a tell:** these are the first names in the model's memory for "a person" and "a company". Plausible invented names are the same tell, only harder to spot.
- **The question:** is this a real client, a real quote, with permission to show it? If not, it breaks the facts-only rule; remove it or label it plainly as an example.

### Invented customers and logos
- **Marker:** a logo wall, "agencies using AVP", customer counts, case-study names or quotes that no record supports.
- **Why it is a tell:** social proof is the most persuasive thing to invent and the easiest to fabricate.
- **The question:** which real, consenting customer is this, and where is that recorded? With none, the section does not ship.

### Round or falsely precise figures
- **Marker:** `99.99% uptime`, `10,000+ teams`, `10x faster`, `100% satisfaction`, or the reverse disguise, `47.3%` with no source on the page. Also uptime, accuracy or "engines covered" claims AVP has not measured.
- **Why it is a tell:** round numbers are what a model writes when it needs a number; odd decimals are what it writes when told to avoid round ones. Neither was measured.
- **The question:** where was this figure measured, and could the page cite it (a scan fact, a database count, `docs/scoring-spec.md`, or a cited paper such as those in `docs/north-star.md` section 9.1)? Unsourced: delete it or relabel it "unverified heuristic".

### Misquoted research figures
- **Marker:** "boosts AI visibility by 40%" or any GEO study figure stated flatly, without its conditions.
- **Why it is a tell:** models remember the headline number and drop the caveat. Aggarwal et al. (arXiv:2311.09735) report gains of up to 40% on GEO-bench, varying by domain. Martinez's survey (arXiv:2607.14035) finds that gain "is conditional on a source already being present in a fixed context and does not establish organic discoverability" (`docs/north-star.md` ~988-1001).
- **The question:** does the sentence carry the paper, its scope and its caveat? Section 9 calls itself web-sourced, informational and less vetted (~974-982): a research figure on a page needs the paper itself and the founder's OK, and never appears on the landing page or pricing as a benefit.

### Fake product drawn in divs
- **Marker:** a hero "screenshot" built from styled boxes: a dashboard, a score card or a task list that renders no real product screen.
- **Why it is a tell:** models cannot see the product, so they draw what a product usually looks like.
- **The question:** does a real screenshot or the real design-system component (rendered from fixture data, labelled as an example) exist for this screen?

## Decorative filler

### Numbered eyebrow
- **Marker:** a zero-padded index above each section title: `00 / INDEX`, `001 · Capabilities`, `04. Process`.
- **Why it is a tell:** numbering reads as editorial rigour, so models number everything.
- **The question:** does the visitor use these numbers to find or cite a section?

### Tile pagination
- **Marker:** counters on tiles like `01 / 4` when all four are visible at once.
- **Why it is a tell:** it copies slideshow chrome onto content that does not page.
- **The question:** can the visitor move between these items, and does the counter change?

### Generic step labels
- **Marker:** `Stage 1 / Stage 2`, `Step 01`, `Phase II` as the title of each step.
- **Why it is a tell:** the label is written before the content is known.
- **The question:** what does the user actually do at each step (run a scan, read the report, apply a fix), and is the number doing work the order does not?

### Word strip at the bottom of the hero
- **Marker:** three or four capitalised words under the hero: `TYPE / FORM / MOTION`, `SCAN · SCORE · SHIP`.
- **Why it is a tell:** a portfolio cliche that fills the space between the call to action and the fold.
- **The question:** do these words link anywhere or say something the headline does not?

### Scroll cue
- **Marker:** `Scroll to explore`, a bouncing arrow or mouse-wheel animation at the bottom of the first screen.
- **Why it is a tell:** it fills the hero's lower edge with a gesture everyone already knows.
- **The question:** is there evidence visitors stop at the first screen?

### Decorative status dots
- **Marker:** a small (often pulsing) coloured dot before nav items, rows or badges.
- **Why it is a tell:** a status light suggests a live system; models add it for colour, with no state behind it.
- **The question:** which real state does this dot report (a scan status, a health check), and what turns it off?

### Pills laid on images
- **Marker:** tags over images: `Brand · 02`, `New`, `Case study`.
- **Why it is a tell:** it layers the look of a tagged archive onto images that carry no tags.
- **The question:** does the label hold information the image or caption does not?

### Middle-dot runs
- **Marker:** three or more items joined by middle dots on one line.
- **Why it is a tell:** the middle dot is the model's default separator for anything that looks like metadata.
- **The question:** is this a real list the visitor scans (for example a `MetaChip` row of scan facts)?

### Decorative grids and crosshairs
- **Marker:** hairline grids, corner crosshairs or registration marks behind content that does not align to them.
- **Why it is a tell:** printer's marks signal craft; over a layout that ignores them they are wallpaper.
- **The question:** does any content sit on this grid, and do the design docs name it?

### Vertical text
- **Marker:** a label rotated ninety degrees along an edge.
- **Why it is a tell:** the fastest way to look experimental.
- **The question:** what does the rotation let the visitor read that horizontal text would not?

### Micro-sentences under eyebrows
- **Marker:** a short sentence between eyebrow and heading, or under the heading, restating the section: "Everything we ship, nothing we promise."
- **Why it is a tell:** every section in the training data looks framed, so models pad each one.
- **The question:** if removed, would the visitor lose a fact?

### Glow and neon
- **Marker:** coloured outer glows on buttons, text or borders (`shadow-[0_0_40px_...]`, neon `text-shadow`).
- **Why it is a tell:** glow reads as energy without saying anything. (Stock colours and off-scale utilities also do not compile in AVP's preset.)
- **The question:** what state does this glow mark that the layout does not?

### Blurred blobs
- **Marker:** large blurred colour shapes behind content (`blur-3xl`, `filter: blur(80px)` on an absolutely positioned element).
- **Why it is a tell:** they fill an empty background with colour without a decision.
- **The question:** what would the section lose without it, and do the design docs name it?

### Perpetual motion
- **Marker:** `animation: ... infinite`, `animate-pulse`, `animate-ping`, `repeat: Infinity` on elements that report no live state.
- **Why it is a tell:** a loop makes a static page look alive.
- **The question:** what real ongoing state does this loop report (a scan in progress), and would a still frame say the same?

## Reflex convergence

### Three equal cards
- **Marker:** a three-column row of cards with the same size, structure and class list: icon, title, two lines.
- **Why it is a tell:** the model's first layout for "features"; three looks complete whatever the content.
- **The question:** does the content really come in three items of equal weight?

### Repeated zigzag
- **Marker:** image left, text right, then the reverse, more than twice in a row.
- **Why it is a tell:** alternation is the cheapest way to make repetition look designed.
- **The question:** does each section call for an image, or is it there to keep the rhythm?

### One layout family everywhere
- **Marker:** every section built from the same arrangement, section after section.
- **Why it is a tell:** a structure that worked once is reused under the same prompt pressure.
- **The question:** where does a section's content differ enough to need another arrangement?

### Bento with an empty cell
- **Marker:** a bento grid with a cell holding only a gradient, a pattern or nothing.
- **Why it is a tell:** the grid shape was chosen before the content was counted.
- **The question:** how many real items are there, and does the shape match?

### Headline with a paragraph floating in the corner
- **Marker:** a large heading on one side and a small paragraph pinned to the opposite top corner, aligned to nothing.
- **Why it is a tell:** it imitates a studio portfolio header.
- **The question:** what does the paragraph align to?

### Hairlines on every row
- **Marker:** `border-t` and `border-b` on each row of a list or table so every line is boxed.
- **Why it is a tell:** rules on every row are the default way to make a list look precise. (AVP's `DataTable` and hairline tokens are documented; judge only ruling that is not from the system.)
- **The question:** does the visitor need to track across columns here?

### Score bars on a full track
- **Marker:** comparison bars as a partial fill over a full track, for features or competitors, on a marketing page.
- **Why it is a tell:** it lifts a dashboard component onto marketing to make claims look measured.
- **The question:** what was measured to produce these lengths, against what maximum? A real scan's `ScoreMeter` or `ShareBar` is not this tell.

### Display face by reflex
- **Marker:** a display face outside its documented role: an editorial serif (Fraunces most often) with one italic accent word, or a heavy grotesque set tight and large, anywhere the design docs do not assign it. A Fraunces italic accent word on the landing page is this tell.
- **Why it is a tell:** models map mood words to a face, so removing one reflex tends to install the other.
- **The question:** does `docs/design-system.md` section 0 "Type" assign this face to this role? Space Grotesk (`font-display`: headings, KPI figures, hero numeral), IBM Plex Sans (`font-ui`) and IBM Plex Mono (`font-mono`, evidence) are AVP's system in those roles; Fraunces (`font-editorial`) only inside `.avp-report`. Any face outside its role, any other face, and Inter (rejected by name in section 2) is flagged.

### Warm paper ground
- **Marker:** a warm off-white ground with near-black ink and a single accent on a page outside AVP's system.
- **Why it is a tell:** it is the current default for "tasteful" and says nothing about the product.
- **The question:** is the ground a design-system token? AVP's paper ground is documented (`docs/design-direction.md`; the report stays paper in both themes), so it is not a tell inside the system.

### Glassmorphism
- **Marker:** translucent panels blurring what sits behind them: `backdrop-blur`, `backdrop-filter: blur(...)`.
- **Why it is a tell:** the quickest way to make a panel look current.
- **The question:** what does seeing through this panel show the user?

### Monospace everywhere
- **Marker:** IBM Plex Mono on labels, navigation, captions and body numbers far beyond evidence and tabular figures.
- **Why it is a tell:** mono reads as technical, so models spread it.
- **The question:** which content is evidence or tabular data, and does the mono stop there?

### Gradient text
- **Marker:** headings filled with a gradient: `bg-clip-text text-transparent`, `background-clip: text`.
- **Why it is a tell:** the quickest way to make a heading look branded.
- **The question:** is this gradient a design-system token, and does every stop pass contrast in both themes?

### U+2014 (em dash) - OPEN FOUNDER DECISION
- **Marker:** the character U+2014 in visible copy, typed or as `&mdash;`, `&#8212;`, `&#x2014;`.
- **Why it is a tell:** models join clauses with it more often than the writers they learned from; at that density it is a signature. Whether that matters for AVP is undecided.
- **The question:** has the founder decided whether AVP copy uses this character? Until then: report as an informational note (count and surface) only, never as a failure, never in `/privacy`, `/terms` or `/crawler` text, and do not edit any away.

## Hollow copy

### Filler verbs
- **Marker:** `Elevate`, `Seamless`, `Unleash`, `Next-Gen`, `Supercharge`, `Empower`, `Revolutionize`, `Unlock` in headings and calls to action.
- **Why it is a tell:** these verbs fit every product, so the model reaches for them when it knows nothing specific.
- **The question:** what does AVP do, in the words an agency would use (scan, score, compare, fix, report)?

### One intent, several labels
- **Marker:** buttons that ask for the same thing under different words: `Get started`, `Start free trial`, `Sign up` on one page.
- **Why it is a tell:** each section is written alone and gets a fresh phrasing.
- **The question:** what is the one action this page asks for, and what does the product call it?

### Poetic section labels
- **Marker:** `From the field`, `Field notes`, `On the bench` over testimonials, articles or updates.
- **Why it is a tell:** the model dresses functional sections in craft vocabulary.
- **The question:** would a visitor looking for this content search for these words?

### Mannered social proof
- **Marker:** `Quietly trusted by`, `In good company`, `Loved by the best` above a logo wall.
- **Why it is a tell:** false modesty is the model's idea of confidence; the adverb carries the claim so the logos do not have to.
- **The question:** are these real clients who agreed to appear? With none, no such heading ships.

### Guaranteed outcomes
- **Marker:** "get cited by ChatGPT", "rank #1 in AI answers", "guaranteed visibility" on the landing page, pricing or report.
- **Why it is a tell:** it is the sales register models default to. the papers cited in `docs/north-star.md` section 9.1 (third-party) say results vary by domain and run; the claim is one the product cannot keep.
- **The question:** does the sentence describe what a scan measures, or promise an outcome?

### Register drift
- **Marker:** tone changes between sections: technical, then lyrical, then salesy in the footer.
- **Why it is a tell:** each section drifts toward the voice its subject most often has in training data.
- **The question:** does every section read in one voice? (Legal pages keep their own register; see `SKILL.md`.)

### Unread strings
- **Marker:** any visible string (heading, label, alt text, button, footer line, error message) nobody read again after writing.
- **Why it is a tell:** one-pass copy keeps the defaults of every family above; they surface on a second read.
- **The question:** has every visible string on this surface been read once more, against the facts-only rule and the docs?
